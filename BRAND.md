# سَنَدي — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name

**Arabic** : سَنَدي
**Latin** : mysanad
**Domain** : mysanad.shop

> *Sanad* (سند) means *support, anchor, trusted backing* in Arabic — a name that already carries the promise of reliability. The pharmacy-grade pivot deepens this meaning : your trusted support for sensitive beauty care.

---

## Identity

**A pharmacy-grade beauty & care house — not a beauty brand.**

Sanadi is positioned as the *trusted KSA pharmacy of selective care*. We don't compete with influencer-driven beauty drops. We compete with the corner pharmacy in Riyadh : same authority, same SFDA discipline, same calm expertise — but only on a tight selection of formulations we believe in.

**Why pharmacy and not beauty :**
- Our products go *inside the body* (drops, powders) or are used on *sensitive zones* (teeth whitening, under-eye)
- KSA buyers reflexively trust pharmacy advice over influencer hype
- The COD model rewards authority and reduces returns
- Margin protection : pharmacy positioning sustains 199–349 SAR price tiers where pure beauty wouldn't

---

## Brand Positioning

> **"عناية بمعايير صيدلية"**
> *Pharmacy-grade care.*

**One-line positioning :**
سَنَدي تختار لك تركيبات نشطة بمعايير صيدلية، مرخصة من هيئة الغذاء والدواء (SFDA)، حلال، وتُسلَّم لباب بيتك مع الدفع عند الاستلام.

**What we are :**
- Curated, not catalogued — only 3 formulations, each solving one clear problem
- Transparent on concentrations and actives (biotin 60,000 mcg, professional LED, strawberry extract)
- SFDA-aligned, halal-certified, Maarouf-verified
- 30-day no-questions guarantee — pharmacist-style assurance, not influencer hype

**What we are NOT :**
- A beauty drop store — we don't follow trends
- A discount catalogue — we don't bundle 12 random products
- A wellness influencer brand — we don't sell "vibes"

**ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) :**
KSA woman, 25–45, urban (Riyadh / Jeddah / Dammam / Khobar), middle-to-upper income. Has tried 2–3 viral beauty drops that didn't deliver. Now wants a quieter, more clinical voice she can trust. Buys vitamins at Nahdi or Al-Dawaa without thinking — Sanadi is the e-commerce equivalent of that habit.

## Voice & Tone

| | Old (beauty empathy) | New (pharmacy authority) |
|---|---|---|
| **Style** | Emotional, sister-like | Calm, expert, pharmacist-like |
| **Reasoning** | "We understand you" | "Here's why it works" |
| **Proof** | Testimonials | Concentrations + SFDA + ingredient mechanism |
| **Hype** | "Transform!" | "Designed for visible support with consistent use" |
| **Voice metaphor** | A trusted friend | A trusted pharmacist behind the counter |

Keep warmth — but lower the emotional volume by ~30 % and raise the expertise volume by ~70 %.

## Color Palette — Pharmacy Authority

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `brand.teal` | **#0A4D4A** | Primary — deep pharmacy teal, near to Nahdi/Al-Dawaa green family |
| `brand.dark` | **#062E2C** | Headers, dark backgrounds |
| `brand.mint` | **#E7F2EF** | Light surface wash, badges |
| `brand.cream` | **#FAFBF9** | Page background (cleaner than warm cream) |
| `brand.sand` | **#F3EEE3** | Reserved — only for premium / certification context |
| `brand.gold` | **#B8862A** | Premium accent, used **sparingly** (certifications, premium tier) |
| `brand.charcoal` | **#0F1B1B** | Primary text |
| `brand.gray` | **#5B6868** | Secondary text |
| `brand.success` | **#0E6B4D** | Confirmations, success states |
| `brand.alert` | **#B42318** | Errors, urgency |

**Rationale :** The deeper teal/green echoes the visual vocabulary of KSA's dominant pharmacy chains (Al-Nahdi, Al-Dawaa). Gold is reduced from decorative to *certificational* — it appears only next to authority markers (SFDA, halal, 30-day seal). Cream warms are replaced by clean whites and a near-white cream, signaling clinical clarity rather than spa-warmth.

## Brand Pillars

1. **صيدلية المعايير** — Pharmacy-grade standards (SFDA, GMP, halal)
2. **تركيبات مختارة** — Selective formulations (only 3 SKUs, each researched)
3. **شفافية الجرعة** — Transparent dosing (visible concentrations)
4. **ضمان ذهبي 30 يوم** — Pharmacist-style 30-day guarantee
5. **سعودي، حلال، مرخّص** — Saudi-rooted, halal, licensed

---

## Don'ts

- Don't use influencer slang ("ترند", "وايرل", "كيوت")
- Don't promise transformation ("في 7 أيام", "بشرة مثل الأطفال")
- Don't add more than 3 SKUs without re-validating the pharmacy thesis
- Don't dilute the gold accent — it must remain rare and certificational
